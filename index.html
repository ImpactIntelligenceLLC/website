The Practice

Impact Intelligence is a boutique data science and technology practice. We build the models, pipelines, and analysis that turn an organization's data into decisions it can defend.

Engagements are sized for mid-market companies, startups, nonprofits, and consulting partners that need senior technical work without a large program built around it. Every engagement is delivered hands-on, from problem framing through the system that runs in production.

About

A single-member practice, led by a machine learning PhD with applied experience across energy and utilities, healthcare equipment, and large-catalog retail operations.

Those are environments where the data is imperfect, the constraints are operational, and a model only matters if the people who rely on it can act on what it says. That shapes how the work gets done: understand the business problem first, keep the method appropriate to it, and leave behind something a client's own team can run.

Engagements range from focused analysis and model development to longer-term advisory work alongside internal teams.
